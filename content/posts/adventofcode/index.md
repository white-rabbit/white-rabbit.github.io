Last year was the first time I participated in [Advent Of Code](https://adventofcode.com/2024). The platform provides a beautifully
minimalistic way to submit your solutions to the daily puzzles which are written in a genuinely hilarious way!


If you want to try, please be prepared to be more pixelated than you remember, hiking near the Lava Production Facility,
participate in a race condition (you can get your own mutex!), control a robot
that controls a robot that controls a robot (that controls a robot)... on the Reindeer class starship.
And of course, be sure you speak monkey fluently before the start!


There are basically two puzzles a day and most of them can be solved quite fast.
But don't be so relaxed, sometimes there is a real challenge over there!

I have no idea how many brains have the ones who are at the top of the ranklist,
I can assume they operate on a teraherz frequency... In my case my goal was to solve both puzzles a day... You know, "it ain't much, but it's honest work" (c) :upside_down_face:

Ironically the most difficult task for me ([Day 21](https://adventofcode.com/2024/day/21)) came on a day I was on a train. :steam_locomotive:
I felt I was in the middle of the crowd of panicking Historians (just like in the task description!).
Additionally I didn't have a laptop, so everything was through an iPad.
As it was extremely inconvenient I had to solve the optimization task twice - what code to write and HOW to do it.
I wonder if I could minimize the number of curse words while I was fixing the infinite amount of
lines with wrong identation (yeah, Python) with my bare fingers...

Eventually it was a nice and refreshing experience to me who usually don't have time to solve algorithmic tasks daily.
