Ok, that was tough! The "publish" button was pressed in the last hour but we did it!

My first entry in [Ludum Dare](https://ldjam.com/events/ludum-dare/43/the-astonishing-airscapade-of-sir-bittergloom)
with the AMAZING team from [General Arcade](https://generalarcade.com/).


The key point of the competition is the topic on which the game needs to be based.
For Ludum Dare 43 it was "Sacrifice must be made".
Indeed we had to sacrifice our sleep!


It's really difficult to come up with an idea that everyone would
agree to work on, but eventually, we found one!  The initial idea was simple:

- you are an aeronaut that must drop off his **first class** belongings to keep the balloon in the air;
- while doing this you additionally fight with physics (do you remember the game about the car and the bear?).

Then after two days (and nights) in [Unity](https://unity.com/) (hello C#) with the team and here it is.

**The Astonishing Airscapade of Sir Bittergloom**
